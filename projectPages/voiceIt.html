VoiceIt

GitHub

Lead developer of VoiceIt — an app that allows users to sign, create, and share petitions completely online.

Presented the app at the #HouseOfCode event in Washington DC 2025 after winning first place in the Oklahoma Congressional App Challenge Demonstration Day hosted by Congresswoman Stephanie Bice.

The Congressional App Challenge is a nationwide coding competition that encourages middle and high school students to create original apps and develop computer science skills.
